# Aomi Matcha Zen (19)

Build an Awwwards-winning, ultra-interactive landing page for a ceremonial-grade Japanese matcha brand called 'Aomi Matcha'. Use React, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, and Lucide Icons.

1. Visual Aesthetic & Japanese Atmosphere:

Theme: High-end Japanese zen minimalism combined with modern luxury design.

Color Palette: Warm off-white/washi paper background (#F7F4EF), Deep Forest Moss Green (#1B2E1E), Soft Sage (#8A9A86), Muted Gold (#C5A059), and dynamic fruit accent colors.

Background Detail: A large, elegant vertical Japanese Kanji watermark '青み' rendered in ultra-subtle translucent opacity in the background.

2. Header & Navigation:

Sleek sticky header: Minimalist logo 'AOMI 青み', nav items ('Ceremonial Grade', 'Flavors', 'The Ritual', 'Our Farm'), and an interactive sliding Cart Drawer trigger button.

Custom magnetic cursor effect that expands slightly over clickable elements.

3. Hero Section & Interactive Arc Carousel:

Centerpiece Glass: A high-definition layered glass cup of iced matcha latte sitting on a natural dark slate stone pedestal.

Floating Curved 3D Arc Carousel: Arrange 5 interactive cards along a smooth curved arc around the bottom of the glass:

Shizuoka Original (Classic Pure Ceremonial Matcha)

Uji Strawberry (Emerald Matcha + Fresh Strawberry Cream)

Kyoto Blueberry (Deep Violet Blueberry Layer + Matcha)

Hokkaido Raspberry (Ruby Red Raspberry Infusion)

Toasted Brown Sugar Crumb (Matcha with Golden Crunch Topping)

4. Micro-Interactions & Transitions:

On Card Select:

Smooth Framer Motion spring rotation to bring the active card front and center.

The liquid layers inside the central cup smoothly morph colors to match the selected flavor.

Ambient background tint shifts seamlessly.

Floating visual elements (leaf particles, fruit slices, ice cubes) drift in 3D parallax around the glass.

Tasting Notes Widget: Display dynamic bar gauges showing Umami, Sweetness, Richness, and Caffeine Level that update smoothly based on the active selection.

Interactive Whisking Widget: An optional mini-interaction section titled 'The Craft' where users can hold down their mouse/finger to 'whisk' matcha powder into a foam, triggering a fluid particle animation.

5. Typography & UI Components:

Serif headlines (like Playfair or Cormorant Garamond style) paired with ultra-clean sans-serif body text.

Glassmorphism floating badges for '100% Ceremonial Grade' and 'Direct from Uji, Kyoto'.

Include a smooth slide-over Shopping Bag side-drawer when clicking 'Order Fresh Batch'.

Ensure 60fps performance, fully responsive design for mobile and desktop.
